# Map data

* `municipalities.json` — derived from geoBoundaries gbOpen TLS ADM1, source: OpenStreetMap, Wambacher,
  licence: Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0 (© OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
  The derived database is shared under the same licence (ODbL 1.0). Ataúro was split from Díli to show the
  municipality created in 2022.
* `posts.json` — derived from geoBoundaries gbOpen TLS ADM2, source: WHO, OCHA ROAP,
  licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Intergovernmental Organisations (CC BY 3.0 IGO) (https://data.humdata.org/dataset/timor-leste-administrative-boundaries-levels-0-3).
  Post boundaries may predate the 2022 changes.
* geoBoundaries: Runfola, D. et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative
  boundaries. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231866. https://www.geoboundaries.org

Simplified with tools/make-map.py; coordinates are WGS84 longitude/latitude.

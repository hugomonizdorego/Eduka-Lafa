# Matadalan Tetun Lafa nian — Tetun style guide

Lafa writes Tetun so that a Timorese learner can read and understand it.
The reference is the official Tetun text of the **Konstituisaun Repúblika
Demokrátika Timór-Leste** (Asembleia Konstituinte, 22 March 2002; Tetun
edition published 2010), which follows the standard orthography of the
Instituto Nacional de Linguística (INL). When the Constitution and everyday
usage differ, Lafa follows the Constitution and notes the alternative.

## 1. Spelling (ortografia)

| Rule | Examples from the Konstituisaun |
|---|---|
| Portuguese loanwords take Tetun spelling: `c/qu → k`, `ç/ss → s` (between vowels `z`), `ção → saun`, `dade` stays, `j/g (soft) → j` | konstituisaun, eleisaun, edukasaun, kompeténsia, sidadaun, prezidente, organizasaun, polítika, ekonomia, Ministériu |
| Mark the stressed vowel when it is not the second-last syllable, and on Portuguese endings `-ál, -ór, -él, -íl` | nasionál, ofisiál, tribunál, podér, provedór, sivíl, repúblika, demokrátika, polítiku, públiku, símbolu, Pátria |
| `ñ` and `ll` as in Portuguese | rekoñese, koñesimentu, Konsellu, traballadór |
| `x` for the "sh" sound | xefe, konxiénsia, Xanana |
| The apostrophe marks the glottal stop | ne'e, ne'ebé, ko'alia, hala'o, la'o, di'ak, to'o, ha'u, nu'udar, kle'an, la'ós, de'it |
| Hyphen in compounds and repeated words | Lei-Inan, lei-oan, rai-laran, tasi-le'un, hotu-hotu, oin-oin, ida-idak, riku-soin, meiu-ambiente, funu-maluk, hela-fatin |
| Possessive with `nia` / `sira-nia`, possessor first | povu nia hakarak, sidadaun sira-nia direitu, Timór-Leste nia independénsia |

Lafa content files use the plain apostrophe `'`; the screen shows it as `’`.

## 3. Dates and numbers

* Months: Janeiru, Fevereiru, Marsu, Abríl, Maiu, Juñu, Jullu, Agostu, Setembru, Outubru, Novembru, Dezembru.
* Days: Segunda, Tersa, Kuarta, Kinta, Sesta, Sábadu, Domingu.
* Short date: **28 Novembru 1975**. Formal (Konstituisaun): *loron 28 fulan Novembru tinan 1975*.
* Numbers: ida, rua, tolu, haat, lima, neen, hitu, ualu, sia, sanulu; atus (100), rihun (1 000), tokon (1 000 000), biliaun.
  Write large numbers with digits and a space or dot: 1 340 434 or 1.340.434.
* Years ago: "tinan 44 000 liubá"; centuries: "sékulu 16".

## 4. Grammar and style

* Short sentences, active voice. One idea per sentence for Ensinu Báziku.
* Word order: subject – verb – object; adjectives after the noun (uma boot, rai foun); numbers after the noun (munisípiu 14).
* Negation: `la` + verb (la iha, la hatene); `la'ós` for "not (it is not)".
* Use Tetun function words, not Indonesian ones: maibé (not *tapi*), ona (not *sudah*), tanba (because), atu (to), ho (with), husi (from), ba (to), iha (in/at/have), ne'ebé (which), wainhira / bainhira (when), tuir (according to), kona-ba (about).
* Plural: `sira` after the noun (sidadaun sira).
* Questions: saida (what), se (who), iha ne'ebé (where), bainhira (when), tanba saida (why), oinsá (how), hira (how many).
* Explain new technical words once in plain Tetun, e.g. "semi-prezidensiál (Prezidente no Governu fahe podér)".

# Content review list (0.2.1)

Lafa does not claim this material is complete or final. The items below were
written from scientific, museum, archive and official sources, but these
facts, links and Tetun terms still need a human check before a teacher relies
on them. The Encarta images given as inspiration were not copied.

Network note: during development the sandbox could not reach wikipedia.org,
wikimedia.org or timor-leste.gov.tl, so links were not checked live. Run
`python3 scripts/check-system.py` or the offline pack on a connected machine.

## Facts and links to verify

## life_environment, science_space, culture_arts
- IPCC AR6 figures: ~1.1 °C warming (2011–2020 vs 1850–1900); ~20 cm sea-level rise 1901–2018; ~410 ppm CO2 in 2019.
- "About 10% energy passes to the next level" — textbook rule of thumb.
- Royal Society founded 1660; Philosophical Transactions 1665.
- Solar System scale model numbers (Sun 22 cm ball; Earth ~24 m; Jupiter ~120 m; Neptune ~700 m) computed.
- Five dwarf planets (IAU 2006 definition).
- ~7,000 languages, ~40% endangered (Ethnologue); >1,200 Austronesian languages; ~1,000 languages in New Guinea; UNESCO Decade of Indigenous Languages 2022–2032.
- Timor-Leste: "more than 30" national languages; Papuan: Fataluku, Makasae, Makalero, Bunak; Austronesian: Tetun, Mambai, Kemak, Tokodede, Galoli, Baikeno, Waima'a.
- Sulawesi cave paintings >40,000 years; rock art in eastern Timor-Leste caves (Lene Hara) — verify.
- Tais inscribed by UNESCO (Urgent Safeguarding List) 2021; reasons summarised generally; source is UNESCO's general lists page.
- URLs to link-check: ethnologue.com/insights/how-many-languages/, education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/food-chain/, undsci.berkeley.edu/
- Tetun coinages to check: "planeta anaun", "dekompozitór", "produtór", "kintura asteroide".
## civilizations, inventions, colonialism
- Kuk (New Guinea): taro and bananas at least 7,000 years ago (conservative).
- Timor archaeology: pottery/domestic animals "last few thousand years"; Austronesian spread "from about 4,000 years ago" (a view).
- Goa printing press by 1556; presses in >200 European towns by 1500 (figures 240–270).
- Timor sandalwood in Chinese writings "centuries before Europeans" (no source named).
- Portuguese contact "around 1515" (sources 1512–1515); Dominicans on Solor 1560s; Dutch at Kupang 1650s (1653); Dili capital 1769; border settled early 1900s (1914 arbitration).
- CAVR: "at least 102,800 conflict-related deaths" — check against Chega!.
- 1999 consultation: turnout ~98%, 78.5% for independence.
- Ghana "one of the first countries south of the Sahara".
- Writing invented independently "at least three times… possibly Egypt".
- URLs to link-check: britannica.com/topic/origins-of-agriculture, whc.unesco.org/en/list/887, un.org/dppa/decolonization/en, peacekeeping.un.org/mission/past/etimor/etimor.htm
- Tetun to check: "surat-tahan", "ai-kameli", "bloku ai", "hanehan", "hakur tasi boot", "To'os-na'in dahuluk sira".
## industrial_revolution, world_wars, geography_oceans
- Ataúro reefs: average 253 reef-fish species per survey site, highest average recorded (Conservation International 2016) — verify.
- Timorese WWII deaths "tens of thousands, estimates often 40,000–70,000" (cited to Britannica; may not contain the figure).
- Ombai–Wetar strait >3,000 m deep; blue whales mostly Oct–Dec; pilot & sperm whales.
- Nino Konis Santana park protects reefs around Jaco; "some villages use tara bandu" on Ataúro.
- Coral Triangle: ~76% of reef-building coral species, >2,000 reef fish species, 6 of 7 sea turtles, >100 million coastal people, Coral Triangle Initiative 2009.
- CO2 ~280 ppm pre-industrial, ~420 ppm 2023 (NOAA).
- Britain ~half urban by 1851; Liverpool & Manchester Railway (1830) first inter-city all-steam timetabled.
- Timor-Leste ~8–9.5°S, 124–127.5°E; Dili ~8.6°S 125.6°E.
- URLs to link-check: conservation.org/timor-leste (least certain), nationalarchives.gov.uk 1833 factory act, climate.gov CO2 page, oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/oceanwater.html, education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/continent/, coraltriangleinitiative.org
- Tetun to check: komboiu, trinxeira, Liga Nasaun sira, Direitu Umanu sira, rekife korál, estreitu kle'an.
## earth, dinosaurs, human_origins
- Period dates ~252–201, ~201–145, ~145–66 Ma (current ICS chart may give ~143 Ma for the Cretaceous base).
- Timor geology: collision "a few million years ago" (3–8 Ma in literature); Australian plate ~7 cm/yr north; Ramelau "close to 3,000 m" (2,963 or 2,986 m); Atauro volcanoes inactive; Baucau plateau raised coral limestone; fossil shells/corals/crinoids studied >100 years; no dinosaur fossils known on Timor.
- Stegodon remains on Timor and Flores.
- T. rex ~12 m; Velociraptor turkey-sized; Chicxulub ~180 km (150–200); ~three-quarters of species extinct; 2004 tsunami >200,000 deaths; Everest 8,849 m (2020); oldest zircons ~4.4 Ga.
- Human–chimp split ~6–7 Ma; out of Africa ~70,000–50,000 years; Denisovan DNA in New Guinea/Australia; Tetun & Mambae Austronesian; Fataluku, Makasae, Bunak Papuan.
- Jerimalai: limestone rock shelter near Tutuala; excavation led by Sue O'Connor (ANU); shell hook "thousands of years younger" than 42,000 years.
- URLs to link-check: nhm.ac.uk/discover/what-is-a-dinosaur.html, nhm.ac.uk/discover/how-are-fossils-formed.html, pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/inside.html, science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1207703, nature.com/articles/nature22336, britannica.com/biography/Nicolas-Steno
- Tetun to check: dinosauru, fatuk-ahu, ahu-ruin (koral), kuak Chicxulub, fatuk-kuak, sientista, Neandertál.
## Timor-Leste: culture, nature, history (full Tetun text — needs a Timorese reviewer)
- Tais: "futus" related to futu (to tie); marriage exchange (bride's side cloth, groom's side animals/goods; customs differ); "tais mane wrapped around the waist by men".
- Uma lulik: belak discs and headdresses as heirlooms; round cone-roofed houses in other regions.
- Dances: bidu described generally; gongs and bamboo flutes; likurai once welcomed warriors; men take part in some regions.
- Lafaek legend: generic outline incl. "ashamed and asked other animals" variant; Lafaek as children's magazine.
- Tetun: INL orthography adopted 2004; Tetun Terik on parts of the south coast and West Timor.
- Nino Konis Santana park: declared 2007 (legal steps 2008); >1,000 km² (~1,236 km²); Nino Konis Santana died 1998, from this region; Lene Hara inside the park.
- Ombai–Wetar >3,000 m; Ataúro 2016 survey record reef-fish counts; macaques & Timor deer probably introduced; Timor friarbird and iris lorikeet as regional endemics.
- Jerimalai shell hook ~16,000–23,000 years; "tens of thousands" of fish bones.
- URLs to check: ich.unesco.org/en/state/timor-leste-TL, datazone.birdlife.org/country/timor-leste, cbd.int/countries/profile/?country=tl, science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1207703; uma lulik, lafaek legend and dance articles rely on general sources only.
- Tetun to check: bird names (pombu matak Timor, pombu boot Timor, pardál Timor, manu-koak Timor, lorikeet íris, kakatua ho fulun kinur iha ulun); baleia kaxalote, golfiñu, dugongu, anzól, atun, rakit, kadiuk, sanan-rai, flauta au, gong, bee-kór, ai belar, suku hamutuk, uma hun, kakuluk; "lian ne'ebé hakotu badak iha kakorok", "dada isin ba kotuk", "Soru ho tali iha kotuk", "fatin forsa", "ahi nia rastu"; "tinan rihun sanulu barak", "maizumenus tinan rihun 16 to'o 23", "liu km² 1.000"; greetings Bondia, Botarde, Bonoite, Di'ak ka lae?, Ha'u di'ak, obrigadu/obrigada, Ita-nia naran saida?; avó (crocodile), funu-na'in.

## Terms a native Tetun speaker should check

- **Rai** for "Save" (save_btn and "Rai ba offline"). This is kept as the task asked, but "rai" also means land or earth. "Salva" may be clearer on buttons.
- **Sentimentu** for "Mood". "Laran" or "Espresaun" are other options.
- **Deskansa** for the idle state. The old text was "Kalmu".
- **planta feto** for ferns (Jurassic text). I don't know the usual Tetun word for fern.
- **tasi-ben** for waves (beach line in personality.py). This was kept.
- **Kaban** for jaw, and **tata-kotu** for "crush bone".
- **ahi-rahun** for soot.
- **Nível / nível**: with the accent, or plain "nivel"?
- **Estinsaun boot** and **habelar-an** (spread out, used for mammals after the asteroid).
- **Leitór ekrán** against a fully Tetun phrase for "screen reader".
- **Zona oras**, **buka ona / haree ona** ("scanned") and **Lupa**. These are short UI labels and were left as they are or only lightly changed.
- **Navegadór** against **browser**: both are still used in the UI. Only the lesson title was changed to navegadór. One of them should be chosen and used everywhere.
- **Kartaun** against **karta** for UI cards (I chose kartaun).
